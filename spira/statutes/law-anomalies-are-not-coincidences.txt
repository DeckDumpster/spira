When a run produces an anomaly that the next pass papers over — an aeon that woke idle while work was ready, a count that came back wrong, a log line that has never appeared before — diagnose it before moving on and file what you find. A symptom that heals itself is still a defect, and self-correction is not evidence of correctness. Treating one as coincidence is how a builder came to exit idle with ninety beads ready while the transient store failure that caused it went unrecorded and unrecoverable.
