A component that replaces an existing authority is built and launched inert; then one batch cuts every caller over while the data migration runs, and the system takes downtime — stop and drain — rather than carrying two authorities, dual writes, a shrinking allowlist or a drift report to keep the old path alive. A design may not spend complexity on a cutover period. Scar: the bead-lifecycle design listed two authorities during cutover as a risk to mitigate rather than a period not to have.
