The harness checkout is production. Every aeon, sentinel and timer reads its scripts from that path as they sit on disk, so an edit left staged or unstaged there is running unreviewed while being invisible to the commit graph — worse than a bad commit, which someone can at least see and revert. Never end a claim or a session with that checkout dirty: commit and push what you changed, or restore it. Scar: an aeon closed sp-a8c5 on a staged lib.sh carrying a real ppid bug, live for hours.
