Every measurement ends as something Ryan can see in Grafana. When a decision, design or fix turns on a measurement (a meter, a threshold, a before-and-after, 'measure it'), the deliverable sends the metric as OTLP into the metrics store and ships a Grafana panel he can open himself; a log line, a Server-Timing header or a number in chat is not the meter. A design that names a measurement names its panel. Keep the path simple, predictable and automatic.
