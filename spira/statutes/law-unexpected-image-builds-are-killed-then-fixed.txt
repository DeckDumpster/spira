An image build nobody expected is a fault, not a slow step: kill it at once, then fix why it started. A run, a gate or a round that finds its image absent stops; it never spends twenty minutes compiling toolchains in the critical path while the work waits. The expected builds are named: the template refresh and an explicit testenv image.
