When a rule is discovered, the deliverable is a guard, not a note. But scope the guard before writing it: grep for every caller of the command you are wrapping, exempt human recipients explicitly, and confirm the guard actually sits on your own PATH. A shim added to block agent mail also severed the watchers' only channel to Ryan, broke a watcher that mails by design, and never bound the interactive agent it was aimed at, because a login shell resolved the command elsewhere. A guard on a shared path binds whoever is most disciplined about using it — usually the automation — and misses the actor who caused the incident.
