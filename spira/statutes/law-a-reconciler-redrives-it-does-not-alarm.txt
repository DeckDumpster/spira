A reconciler redrives; it does not alarm. Components emit wide events and counters for what went wrong, and alarms are rules over that telemetry downstream, never logic baked into the reconciler or the component. Work a reconciler cannot complete stays undone in its store for the next redrive. Scar: the runtime's offload spillover alarm was specified inside the reconciler (per Ryan 2026-10-28, sp-vvow3v).
