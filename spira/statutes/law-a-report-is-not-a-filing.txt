A finding exists only where something will surface it: an open bead, an insight, or an ask in the operator's pane. A close reason and a chat report are not destinations — both are read only by someone already looking for them. Put the decision in the pane and the finding in a bead, then let the report say what you filed. Scar: a real defect closed into a close reason with a promised refile survived only because an unrelated commit happened to fix it.
