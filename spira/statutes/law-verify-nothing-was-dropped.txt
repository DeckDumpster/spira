After resolving a merge conflict, diff every line the other side added against your result and confirm none went missing. Taking --theirs on a rebase looked plainly right and silently dropped 27 lines of a fix that had merged an hour earlier, including the whole function it added; the next red run would have read as a fresh flake rather than a revert. The same check on the next PR flagged one line and proved it a deliberate renumbering. Only the check distinguishes them.
