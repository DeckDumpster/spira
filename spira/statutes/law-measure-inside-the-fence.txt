Measure a timeout or budget inside the fence that will kill it, and check the fence in the same change that moves the budget. A number calibrated outside its own cgroup, or set above its own systemd ceiling, is not a budget — the Loom query measured 157 ms unfenced and 712 ms under the CPUQuota=20 percent its unit sets, and spira-suites has run an 1800 s budget under TimeoutStartSec=900 and never once succeeded. Re-measure inside the cgroup, then set the fence above the measurement. (sp-cbjh, sp-2atr, sp-xjbd)
