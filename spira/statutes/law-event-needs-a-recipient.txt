Every event a producer emits names the persona that will discharge it, and that persona must be able to perform the fix the event admits. A producer with no such recipient does not emit — it refuses. The operator receives decisions and nothing else: not alerts, not recurrences, not status. An unclaimable-bead incident addressed to Ops, a persona chartered not to repair, fired 201 times in twelve hours and reached the operator only as a page asking whether to mute it.
