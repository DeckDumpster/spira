Landed is not in force. A change takes effect only when the executing copy runs it: the commit is an ancestor of the production checkout's HEAD, and the installed unit's ExecStart matches what its template renders today. Verify both before asserting a behaviour changed, and make a promotion or install path that cannot advance escalate rather than exit non-zero into a journal. Scar: a stray commit diverged the prod checkout and froze nineteen landings while a timer failed silently every two minutes.
