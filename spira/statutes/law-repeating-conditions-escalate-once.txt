A condition that stays true escalates once. A timer that re-sends while nothing has changed teaches the operator to filter the sender, and that filter is how the one message that mattered gets missed. Stamp an escalation on the identity of the condition, not on the pass that noticed it; re-send only when the facts change, and never more than once per window. Prefer filing a bead to mailing a person for anything they cannot decide. Scar: the sentinel's requeue cap mailed the operator every two minutes for hours — twenty identical mails about two beads, none of them actionable even once.
