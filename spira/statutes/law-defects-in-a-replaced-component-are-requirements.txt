A defect found in a component slated for replacement is recorded first as a REQUIREMENT of its replacement, on that replacement's design, never as a bead that fixes the doomed code. A defect bead already open against such a component is closed, with its requirement carried over. Filing the fix spends a builder, a batch and a CI run on code that is about to be deleted.
