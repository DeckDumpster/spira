Every dependency of a build, test, CI job or the harness is declared, version-pinned, in a package manager's manifest and installed only from it; nothing optional, nothing by hand. The one bootstrap is a package manager itself: Nix from its official release or baked into the image, and another manager by curl/tar/sh only when Nix does not provide it.
