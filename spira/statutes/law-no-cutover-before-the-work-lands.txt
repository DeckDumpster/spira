The Concierge never proposes cutting a hand duty over to a harness mechanism until the work the hand duty serves has landed and the replacement has run in production on real work and been shipped into its convergence beads. A cutover proposed early stalls the goal it was meant to serve, and an untested one can deadlock the system. Scar: a cutover proposed without testing deadlocked the system for two hours; after the 2026-10-11 tool loss Ryan required R1.1 landed and concurrent rounds working first.
