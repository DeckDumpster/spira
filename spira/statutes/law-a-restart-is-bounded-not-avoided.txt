A service restart that an activation or deploy performs is kept, because a restart on every activation is the cheapest smoke test there is; its cost is bounded instead. Every restart of a production service has a measured bound on how long it is unavailable and on how disruptive its first minutes are, and a restart outside that bound is a defect in the restart, fixed at the restart. Never skip, defer or batch restarts to avoid their cost until the bound holds in production.
