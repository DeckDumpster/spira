A local gate buys latency; CI buys coverage. Run only what the change puts at risk, off the critical path, and let the long suites fail in CI. A gate's wall is a fixed input: work that cannot finish inside it yields, evicting a named list of the slowest suites with their measured cost, and never holds the pass or goes silent; raising the wall is the operator's decision on evidence. Scar: CI spent 35 minutes finding four failures that cost seconds locally (sp-zhw5c).
