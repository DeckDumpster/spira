Every workflow Spira runs (a bead's life, a round and its passes, the VM pool, an aeon's session and closeout, a gate, Sift, a release) is a state machine with fine-grained states and recorded transitions, kept by the component that owns it. Observe it only through that machine's reads and drive it only through its verbs: never by scraping its logs or files, matching its processes, or killing them. A step that cannot be seen or done through the interface is a missing state or verb, so build it. Scar: a round's phase, progress and preemption were read from log lines and done by killing processes in order, and the pane showed 'fences' for four minutes that were a host build and a VM wait (per Ryan, 2026-10-09).
