Every request/response call (a query, a socket RPC, a CLI lookup) carries a client timeout of at most 5 s, and the receiver stops working 3 s after receiving it. A query over 1 s is a defect, never absorbed by a larger timeout. Batch jobs are not calls; starting one returns a handle at once. A model API call carries its own per-pass timeout from config (sp-cayg4m). Scar: an 85 s spira-lc list behind a 60 s timeout stopped every claim (sp-c3azm).
