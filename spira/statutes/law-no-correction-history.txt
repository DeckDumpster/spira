Write documents as self-contained hardened statements. Never 'Corrected on DATE', 'Superseded by', 'DEMOTED', or a body that states a wrong thing and then patches it — the superseded instruction stays in place underneath the warning, and the warning loses. State the rule as it stands now and keep a scar only as the one-clause reason the rule exists. Dated '(per Ryan, YYYY-MM-DD)' provenance and DOC/SRC/OBS verification marks are fine, since those establish a claim rather than retract one.
