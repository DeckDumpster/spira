A filer that deduplicates must publish its duplicate count. Any component keying work on a fingerprint — an incident, an escalation, an alert — measures how many keys were filed more than once and hands a nonzero count to Ops; a probe that cannot read renders ?, never 0. Dedup that is trusted and never measured fails silently: incident.sh queried only open beads, so every close re-filed the bead, and 38 duplicates over two days were found by the operator reading his own queue, not by us.
