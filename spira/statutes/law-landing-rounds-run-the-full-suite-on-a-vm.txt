A landing round lands only when every suite has passed, in the testenv container, against an input closure identical to the landing tree's: a verdict is keyed per suite by the inputs the test plan says it reads, never by the whole tree, so an eject or re-cut re-runs only the suites whose inputs changed; an uncomputable closure re-runs. The container runs on this host, one round at a time, from a warm target; a round whose host slot is taken, or whose host io or cpu pressure is over its threshold, runs on a round VM instead (the spill). Every round records its place, phase times and verdict class. A budget that would drop a covering suite refuses. Scar: tree-keyed verdicts re-ran ~334 suites per eject (sp-ipwlic); VM-only reds that were green under testenv on the same tree stopped landing (sp-lqc2zv).
