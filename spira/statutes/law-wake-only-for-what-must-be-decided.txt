Send the operator only what he must decide, and wake a session only for what it must act on: a red, a failed landing, a real fault, an ask. A landing notice, a PR opening or closing, a publish backlog and a detector's false alarm are records, filed where they are read on demand, never unread mail and never a wake. Actionable events still arrive in real time through the durable inbox, never by keystrokes into a pane. Scar: five days of operator mail held nothing that mattered.
