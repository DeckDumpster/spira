Never cap or deprioritise CPU as a control: no CPUQuota, no Nice. The operating system time-shares well; an explicit quota or nice level on a task has only ever stalled the work behind it — on 2026-09-29 three quotas (landing 70%, test Dolt 50%, production Dolt 100%) each became the loop's bottleneck while the host sat idle. Work greedy enough to starve its neighbours runs in its own VM, as rounds do on the round VM (per Ryan, 2026-09-29).
