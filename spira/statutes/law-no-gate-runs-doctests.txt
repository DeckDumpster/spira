No repository's gate runs doctests. A gate, build tier or test tier that invokes cargo test --doc, in its own step or inside another, deletes that call rather than timing, splitting or reporting it. The commit that deletes it says how many doctests the workspace had and what they covered, so a deletion is never silent. Scar: the ruling 'doc tests are nonsense, why do we even have that' removed one repository's doc-test tier (sp-l058i8), and the next night a draft for a second repository gave the doctests their own timed step and was corrected by hand (sp-xkhvmm; sp-njeocu).
