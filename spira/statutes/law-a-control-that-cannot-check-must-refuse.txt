A control that cannot perform its check must refuse, not warn and proceed. Warn-and-continue turns a safety mechanism into a log line: the run goes ahead unprotected while the warning scrolls past inside a step that passes, so the control sits dead for months reporting nothing wrong. Make an unmet precondition — a missing permission, credential or reachable endpoint — a hard failure that names what to grant. Scar: a CI capacity cap that could not read node memory cloned anyway, filling the hypervisor until guests were killed mid-job.
