Every bead is written by Skuld or reviewed by Skuld before it is filed, whoever files it; a body no Skuld pass has seen is not filed. Skuld is where the acceptance standards live, so a bead that skips it skips them: an acceptance a builder cannot meet in minutes, a measurement that runs for an hour, a target nobody checked. A builder that sees its first measurement miss the target by a wide margin stops and reports; it does not repeat the measurement.
