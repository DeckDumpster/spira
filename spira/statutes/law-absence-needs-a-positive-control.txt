A check that finds nothing must first prove it could have found something. An empty glob, an empty query, a silent grep and a check pointed at the wrong place are indistinguishable from the outside, and the wrong one reads as all-clear — which displaces the suspicion that would have prompted a look. Before reporting absence, resolve a case known to be present, or refuse rather than return zero. Hardcoding a runtime path instead of sourcing the library that defines it reported no workers while two were working.
