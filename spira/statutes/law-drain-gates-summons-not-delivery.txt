world.sh drain stops new aeons and nothing else: the loop, landing, rounds and publishing to the forge all continue, because what publishes is work that already landed and passed its round. Only world.sh stop (halt) stops forge writes. Holding publishes under a drain only batches them into one larger, riskier PR later. If forge writes ever need stopping without a halt, that is a separate switch, never a meaning added to drain (per Ryan, 2026-09-29, sp-26apw).
