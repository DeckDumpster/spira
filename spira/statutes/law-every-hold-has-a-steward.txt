A held bead is never left to sit. A reconciliation loop visits every hold on a schedule and takes one corrective step toward resolving it: it lifts an expired wait, sends the question for an ask that has no mail, routes a stale poison to its diagnosis, and reminds the holder of an old manual hold. Static stability comes from that loop, not from making each write atomic. Scar: on 2026-10-07 an audit found 50 held beads, of which 28 poisons were unattributable, 10 waits were hours past expiry, and 3 asks had never been mailed to anyone.
