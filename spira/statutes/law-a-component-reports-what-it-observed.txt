A component reports what it observed, never the cause it infers: a refused or absent socket is "not running", not "restarting", and no caller branches on a lifecycle state of another component that it cannot observe. The caller acts on the observation (retry within its own deadline, then fail by that name) and leaves the cause to whatever can see it.
