A harness that reports a failure must carry that failure's own explanation. Never discard a subprocess's stderr, and never capture only the tail of a run's output: a helper's 2>/dev/null and a tail-only dump window are one defect, because an empty failure does not withhold a diagnosis, it manufactures a wrong one downstream. Scar: test helpers that discarded stderr hid 'neither SPIRA_HOME nor SPIRA_RELEASE is set' for three rounds while a tail-50 window hid mail's FATAL line for two; on 2026-10-06 the gate kept no output for its harness faults, so every suites composition failed for a missing SPIRA_TOML with nothing saying so.
