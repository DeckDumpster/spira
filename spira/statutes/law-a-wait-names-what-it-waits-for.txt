A wait or barrier names what it waits for — the beads, runs, processes or signal — and its duration is only a cap on that wait, never the condition. Nothing opens or proceeds because a clock ran out: a gate lifts on the event or by an explicit act, and at the cap the waiter acts on the named stragglers (gives up naming them, or slays them by name).
