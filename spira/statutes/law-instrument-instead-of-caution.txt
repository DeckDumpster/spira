Do not buy safety with a delay or a sign-off when telemetry can buy it. A change, a remedy or a rollout acts at once, provided it records when it acted, what it changed and from what state, and measures whether it had the effect it was for. A change that did not have its effect is surfaced with both readings. A soak period or a per-step approval needs a stated reason why measurement cannot catch the failure, such as an irreversible action on production data.
