A bead that names a target closes only when the target is met, shown by the measurement it names, or contradicted, shown by evidence that it cannot be met as stated. A dwarf may give up, but its bead may not: before it stops short it files the follow-on bead that carries the remaining distance, or escalates the target as a decision, and the close names which. Scar: the sim-world bead targeted under 10 s, closed at about 37 s with 'the world is still above 10 s', and nothing was filed.
