Never assert that anything runs because systemctl says a unit is active. A timer reads active while the service it fires fails on every attempt, and a unit is enabled whether or not it was ever started. Measure the outcome instead — Result, ExecMainStatus and NRestarts on the SERVICE, or the artefact each pass writes and its age — and name the pass you saw. Fourteen units ExecStarted from a path that did not exist, failing 203/EXEC for 33 minutes, while every timer read active and the session reported all units green. (Ryan, 2026-09-08, sp-x6dw)
