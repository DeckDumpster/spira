A core process of the loop reads lifecycle, round and config state in-process through the store's and config's libraries, never by spawning another Spira binary or a shell bootstrap: a spawned read pays a whole start-up for one answer, so a probe that forks forty times a minute turns a 40 ms lookup into a second. A child process is for an effector at the edge (ssh, podman, systemctl, the forge), never for a read.
