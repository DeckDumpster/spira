Alarm on what the system exists to do, not on component health. Pokebot bought nothing for 14 days while the daemon was up, warmers were warm and signals parsed at 117/day — every component green, and the only thing wrong was the thing the system is for. Condition the check on reaching the terminal stage so healthy rejections stay quiet, give it a sample floor, treat a gate rejecting nearly everything that reaches it as broken rather than strict, and prove the check fires by replaying the window where the bug was live. Before adding telemetry, check whether the answer is already in a log, an artifact or a health endpoint nobody reads.
