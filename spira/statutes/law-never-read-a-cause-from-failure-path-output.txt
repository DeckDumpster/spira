A line that appears in a failing run is not its cause until it is shown absent from a passing one. Before naming a cause, check the same line in a green run of the same job, and the wiki's known-harmless list (e.g. ci-runner-vm-hazards). A warning present in both is noise, not a diagnosis. Say what the failure path printed, and separately what you verified caused it. Scar: 'pve-ca-cert' — documented harmless — was reported to Ryan as the cause of two unrelated failures, fifteen hours after this rule was proposed (sp-r5bbp, sp-sd7r6, 2026-09-28).
