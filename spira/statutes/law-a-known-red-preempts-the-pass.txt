A pass's reds are watched as they land, not read at its end. The first red does not stop the pass: it starts attribution, and the round is reconfigured beside the running pass (attributable members ejected, the next head staged). Once that reconfigured round is ready, the running pass is preempted (stopped cleanly, results salvaged, VM released) and the new one takes its place.
