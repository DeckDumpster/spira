A harness check may DETECT that what is installed has drifted from its source; it may not repair the drift. Installing units, deploying binaries or otherwise changing what is installed on the operator's machine is an explicit act he orders, never one a timer takes — report and stop. Restarting a unit so a running process matches its already-installed code stays inside that authority. Asked and answered three times: sp-cp6, sp-r69h, sp-jo6f (2026-09-07).
