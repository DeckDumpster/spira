When the box congests, lower the number of concurrent workers — dwarves, agents, gate slots — and let each run at full speed. Never throttle the job itself: no CPU quota, no cut to cargo jobs, suite width or build parallelism. A throttle slows every run including the ones running alone, hides the cause, and has repeatedly caused more trouble than the load it answered. Scar: 2026-09-30, halving per-job limits under 14 herded agents halved landing throughput.
