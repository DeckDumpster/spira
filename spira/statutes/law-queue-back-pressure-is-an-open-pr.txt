Under queue.forge the merge queue's only back pressure is whether a batch PR is open: cut when none is, land when that PR's own gate is green. Under queue.local a round lands locally and an open publish PR never holds the next round. Nothing downstream (main's push CI, a release job, a deploy) may hold, delay or gate staging; watch it as detection that files an Ops bead. Scar: a hold on main's push gate made every batch wait ~20 minutes (sp-x54re, per Ryan 2026-09-24; queue.local scope per the approved local-main design, 2026-09-27).
