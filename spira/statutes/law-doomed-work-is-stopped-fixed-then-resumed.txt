Work the Concierge can see is doomed — a builder rebasing a member its open integration will redo, a round member whose tree cannot pass setup, a bead stacked on a parent that will not land first — is stopped at once: ejected from its round, or slain and held with a manual hold. The defect that doomed it is then filed or amended as an express fix, and the held work is resumed when that fix lands. Letting doomed work run to completion spends builder slots and round passes on output that is thrown away.
