A stalled landing flow starts diagnosis and repair; it does not halt on a daily rate. Every lifecycle edge has an owner that beats and acts when the edge's bound expires, and an edge pages only when its owner is silent or reports a failed action. No edge bound exceeds two hours, each is longer than its owner's silence window, and a scheduler whose tick has gone stale is itself a stall, found in-tree. Scar: a daily-rate floor halted landing on a slow day instead of finding the stuck edge (sp-qlliks); three edges paged on their bound alone because nothing beat for them.
