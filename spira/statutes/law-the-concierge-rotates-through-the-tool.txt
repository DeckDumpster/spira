The concierge is one conversation at a time, reachable from desk and phone: one claude process in the concierge tmux socket with Remote Control. Never start a second client on it. A live session may be replaced by a fresh one only through rotate.sh, which refuses while round work is a child of the session, writes the handoff, archives the old session id and wakes the new session on it; a dead process is resumed on the recorded id. Scar: a 257 MB session wrote ~44 GB in a day.
