Drift between this box and origin is allowed but must shrink. Every local change — a hand edit, a concierge fix, an aeon branch — either reaches origin through the merge queue or is rolled back locally; nothing sits uncommitted or unsubmitted in the live checkout. Never close drift by splitting off a prod clone.
