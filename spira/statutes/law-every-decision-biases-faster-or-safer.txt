Every decision in the system biases toward making development faster or safer, and a choice that makes it neither is the wrong choice. Faster and safer are not in tension here: a slow, many-step path is itself a safety risk, because every extra step, check and retry is another place to fail, and a stalled loop invites bypasses. When two paths are equally safe, take the faster; when one is faster and the other no safer, take the faster. (sp-2gatvz; 2026-10-31: five hours of stopped landing spent on careful indirect steps)
