DESCRIPTION

  ## Intent                                                                   
                                                                              
  The hourly flake observer must never quarantine a suite that CI already     
  judged a real failure, and must never quarantine anything without filing the
  bead that will un-quarantine it.                                            
                                                                              
  ## What happened                                                            
                                                                              
  PR 56 (queue 20260917T173304Z) went red on 2026-09-17. The gate did the     
  right thing: it re-ran the five red suites serially, three went green (real 
  flakes), and two were red twice:                                            
                                                                              
    gate-retry: red twice: test-cockpit-landed.sh test-cockpit-probe-fault.sh 
    gate: suites failed (batch rc=1)                                          
                                                                              
  Fourteen minutes later the local observer quarantined all five anyway, at   
  threshold 1, filing nothing:                                                
                                                                              
    18:00:13Z auto-quarantine: test-suites-result-files.sh quarantined (bead: 
  (none filed))                                                               
    18:00:15Z auto-quarantine: test-doctor-embedded.sh quarantined (bead:     
  (none filed))                                                               
    18:00:17Z auto-quarantine: test-cockpit-probe-fault.sh quarantined (bead: 
  (none filed))                                                               
    18:00:18Z auto-quarantine: test-cockpit-mouse.sh quarantined (bead: (none 
  filed))                                                                     
    18:00:20Z auto-quarantine: test-cockpit-landed.sh quarantined (bead: (none
  filed))                                                                     
                                                                              
  test-cockpit-landed.sh and test-cockpit-probe-fault.sh are the two suites   
  that detect the sp-d0j1g regression. With them off, the next batch carrying 
  sp-d0j1g would have gone green and landed a cockpit whose UNLND/QUEUE keys  
  no suite covers. This is law-never-disarm-a-check-to-proceed, executed by   
  machinery rather than by a person.                                          
                                                                              
  The concierge restored spira/suite-state by hand; the diff is salvaged at   
  $HOME/spira/run/reaped/quarantine-20260917T1800/suite-state.diff       
                                                                              
  ## Three defects, all in scope                                              
                                                                              
  1. **A red-red suite is not a flake.** The observer counts observations in a
  window without reading the gate's own red/green/red-red classification,     
  which is in the same result files it is reading. A suite the retry re-ran   
  and found red again must be excluded from quarantine outright.              
  2. **Threshold 1 is not a threshold.** 1 flake observations in the window   
  (threshold 1) quarantines on a single sighting. One red run should never    
  disarm a check.                                                             
  3. (bead: (none filed))** must be fatal.** A quarantine with no bead is a   
  check that is off with nobody accountable for turning it back on. If the    
  bead cannot be filed, do not quarantine.                                    
                                                                              
  Additionally: the observer writes spira/suite-state inside the production   
  harness checkout, which leaves that checkout dirty on every quarantine (law-
  the-harness-checkout-is-production). Whether the fix here is to commit it   
  through the gate or to move the transitions out of the tree is the          
  implementer's call, but a quarantine must not leave the checkout dirty.     
                                                                              
  ## Acceptance                                                               
                                                                              
  • A suite classified red-red by the gate retry is never auto-quarantined; a 
  suite classified red-green may be, subject to the threshold.                
  • The quarantine threshold is greater than 1 and settable.                  
  • A quarantine that cannot file a bead does not happen, and says why.       
  • A quarantine leaves the harness checkout clean.                           
  • Regression tests, seen red first (law-a-regression-test-must-be-seen-to-  
  fail), driven from real result files for a run with one red-green and one   
  red-red suite.
