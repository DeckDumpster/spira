  The exit status is carried out of the subshell through a file written on the
  line *after* the call. The literal string empty is the cat fallback, so     
  rc=[empty] means $DETECT_FILE did not exist at all — the subshell died      
  before reaching the printf. That is what testdb_up doing exit 75 rather     
  than return 75 looks like from outside, and it is indistinguishable here    
  from                                                                        
  testdb_up returning the wrong code. The assertion cannot tell "exited 75"   
  from "never got to record anything", and reports the second as a failure of 
  the                                                                         
  first.                                                                      
                                                                              
  It is also silent about its own breakage: both 2>/dev/null redirections     
  throw                                                                       
  away whatever the subshell said on the way out.
