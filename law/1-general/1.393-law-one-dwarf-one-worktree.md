# §1.393 law-one-dwarf-one-worktree

No two sessions share a worktree or a branch. A bead's branch is its own, a split never copies the parent's branch onto a child, and a call that finds its branch checked out elsewhere fails loudly instead of moving into the tree that holds it. Nothing but the tree's own session may commit, reset or clean in it. Three sessions once shared one worktree and a sibling's git reset silently dropped another's committed fix, which was caught only by diffing against a remembered commit hash.
