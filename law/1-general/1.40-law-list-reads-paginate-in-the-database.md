# §1.40 law-list-reads-paginate-in-the-database

A list operation paginates in the database, never in the application: its limit, cursor and filters are part of the query, so a page costs a page. A reader that materialises the whole collection and pages in memory pays the full store for every page, and a caller walking the collection pays it squared. Scar: 2026-11-03, 'list --state … --limit 100 --after' on the control store filtered in Rust after reading every bead; 123 pages read 115 GB and admin-parity could not finish (sp-zjkgeh).
