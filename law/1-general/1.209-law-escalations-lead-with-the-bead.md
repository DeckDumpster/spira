# §1.209 law-escalations-lead-with-the-bead

An escalation opens with what the work is FOR, then what went wrong. A log tail answers 'what broke' and cannot answer 'what was this trying to do', which is the question that must be settled before any decision about it means anything. Carry the bead's title, status, age, labels, full description and latest notes, then the evidence. Never build an ask's title from an identifier that may not exist; a subject line ending in a bare dash is a bug reaching Ryan.
