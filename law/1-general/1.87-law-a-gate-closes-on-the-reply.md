# §1.87 law-a-gate-closes-on-the-reply

A bead that exists to wait on Ryan's answer closes only when his answer is on record: his verdict on the ask, or his mail reply. The close cites it. Its own session never closes it on a marker, a timeout or a hope, because closing lifts every dependency behind it and the work proceeds as if he had answered.
