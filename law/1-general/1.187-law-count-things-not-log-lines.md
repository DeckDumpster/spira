# §1.187 law-count-things-not-log-lines

Count the things, not the lines that describe them. An instrument that reads a log must reduce to a SET of the entities it reports — branches, beads, incidents, intervals — never a tally of matching lines, and must never count one event twice by matching both the actor's own line and a later layer's summary of it. An inflated count reads as a backlog and sends someone hunting work that does not exist: 184 fiends were 2 branches, held 221 were 23, and recurrence 5 was 50 quiet minutes. (sp-nef2, from sp-1smg and sp-txew)
