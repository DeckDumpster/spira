# §1.372 law-third-party-tools-are-official-releases

A third-party tool the harness installs or runs — a database server, the beads CLI, a container engine, any dependency deps.toml names — comes only from an official tagged release of its upstream, never a mainline, branch, nightly or locally built commit. Install and upgrade pick the release; a build whose version cannot be traced to a published release tag is drift, reported as such even when its number looks right. Scar: on 2026-10-12 production ran bd '1.2.1 (dev: 634cbbc)', a mainline build, and dolt 2.2.3 while 2.4.2 was released with the cancellation and push-wedge fixes it lacked (per Ryan, 2026-10-12: 'remember you need to use official releases not just random mainline commits').
