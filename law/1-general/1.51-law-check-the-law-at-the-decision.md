# §1.51 law-check-the-law-at-the-decision

A dwarf checks the law at the decision it governs. Before introducing a new program, service, language, file type, dependency or protocol, it runs statute-check on its plan, reads the full text of what returns, and records that on the bead. A statute seen only as a slug in a list is not in force at the moment of choice. Scar: a builder saw the rule on new subsystems as one slug among hundreds and did not read it.
