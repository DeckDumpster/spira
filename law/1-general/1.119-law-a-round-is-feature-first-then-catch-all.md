# §1.119 law-a-round-is-feature-first-then-catch-all

Never let submitted beads idle without a round running. Cut rounds from the SUBMITTED pool directly; no per-bead gate is a precondition, since the round's full suite is the stronger trial and reds are bisected. Feature-focused when a feature is waiting, else a catch-all of every unheld submitted bead. Start N+1 the moment N lands; eject reds beside the next round; never send a known red to CI. Scar: 41 beads idled SUBMITTED behind per-bead gates.
