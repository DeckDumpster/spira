# §1.151 law-arm-before-you-retire

Arm the replacement before you retire the workaround, in the same turn, never after. A watcher, tail or poller you stop is a channel gone silent, and silence from a dead channel is indistinguishable from silence because nothing happened. If the replacement cannot be armed now, keep the workaround running until it can, and say so. Ryan had to report unread verdicts himself after a log-tail was stopped and its Monitor never attached.
