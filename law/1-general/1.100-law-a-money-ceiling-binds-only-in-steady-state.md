# §1.100 law-a-money-ceiling-binds-only-in-steady-state

A money ceiling binds a system in steady state and never one in bring-up: while a capability is still being proved, spend what the task needs, report what it cost in the same message, and restore the ceiling when the operator calls it steady. Never make him wait on a period reset, nor ask him to lift a ceiling you set yourself, to save a sum smaller than the hour it costs him. A time budget stays a cutoff (law-a-budget-is-a-cutoff); money is the one that waits for steady state. Scar: 2026-11-17, a two-dollar daily ceiling stopped a reading run he wanted finished that evening, twice (sp-yb6ltf).
