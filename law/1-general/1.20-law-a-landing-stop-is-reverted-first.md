# §1.20 law-a-landing-stop-is-reverted-first

When landing stops, revert the last change that landed before it, by hand, within 15 minutes, then diagnose; if the active release carries the breakage, roll it back. A cutover is the exception: when the old release cannot run the loop either, fix forward on the live one, since rolling back hides the fault and costs a second activation. (sp-1qi6zt; sp-r1gmno: the bbea2fa75 rollback was undone in minutes)
