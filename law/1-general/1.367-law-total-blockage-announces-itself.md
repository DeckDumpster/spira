# §1.367 law-total-blockage-announces-itself

When a condition stops ALL work — a drain nobody lifted, a stale pause, no account with capacity — escalate once, even when it heals itself: one bead keyed on the condition, with --moot-when so it withdraws the moment the condition clears; never one per pass, never announce the recovery. A stall an ACTIVE capacity pause explains is not escalated — escalate only what nothing explains, or what outlasts a full window, because a window that keeps resetting hides a blockage that is no longer routine. Silence while blocked is the failure: on 2026-09-09 three stalls ran for hours while every pass logged goal reached.
