# §1.14 law-the-harness-switches-his-cockpit

Whatever puts a design, diff or report in front of Ryan switches his cockpit to it itself, every iteration; it never asks him to switch, and only he dismisses it. The switch lands alone: never in a turn whose chat carries anything he must read, so say it in one line and switch on the next turn. Scar: he closed a review hunk-open flipped in mid-read, which killed its comment watcher (per Ryan, 2026-10-13, sp-t2t36f; 2026-10-30).
