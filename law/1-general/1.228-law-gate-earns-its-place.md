# §1.228 law-gate-earns-its-place

A check may sit between work and its landing only while it is catching real defects. Record every gate red as a real defect or a fault of the gate itself, and delete a check whose reds are mostly its own — on the evidence, not after an outage. The 43-suite gate ran seventeen minutes a branch for weeks, found zero real defects on the morning it mattered, produced two failures that were its own suites reading the box, and cost twelve hours. Nobody could have known.
