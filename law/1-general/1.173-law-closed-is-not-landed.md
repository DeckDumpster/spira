# §1.173 law-closed-is-not-landed

CLOSED does not mean landed. Verify with 'git merge-base --is-ancestor <commit> origin/<branch>', never by comparing tip SHAs, because a tip moves under you mid-epic. Re-run 'git fetch' immediately before asserting that anything did NOT land; 'gt orphans' is the built-in hunt. Apply this after an epic finishes, never during — mid-flight, 'not landed yet' and 'never going to land' look identical, and checking early has already produced a false alarm.
