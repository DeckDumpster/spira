# §1.115 law-a-repo-is-defined-in-its-own-terms

A repository's code, docs and checks are written in its own domain's terms and never name another repository or product, not even to forbid it: a check that lists a sibling's vocabulary couples the two repos and defines one's correctness by the other. A fork removes the parent's content outright rather than guarding against it. Scar: dumpstar shipped a banned-names check enumerating pokedumpster's identity and Pokémon vocabulary.
