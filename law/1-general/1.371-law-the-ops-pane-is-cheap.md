# §1.371 law-the-ops-pane-is-cheap

The ops pane renders fast, almost free, and failureless: every refresh reads only the lifecycle's read model and caches anything that changes slower than the refresh, completes in under a second on an idle box, and shows no source failure while the system is healthy. A source that fails or times out on a healthy system is a defect to fix at its source, never a timeout to raise. Scar: the pane called work list (3 s, 459 of its 482 rows stale) under a 5 s cap and showed 'source failed: work list --json: exit 124' for days while the read model built to replace it sat landed and unused.
