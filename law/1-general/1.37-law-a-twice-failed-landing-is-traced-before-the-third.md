# §1.37 law-a-twice-failed-landing-is-traced-before-the-third

When a landing has failed twice, the third attempt is preceded by two read-only adversarial tracers — one on the VM path (the merged tree under the INSTALLED round-vm), one on the host landing and activation path — that predict the failures before they happen, each with file:line evidence and the preventive change. They run in parallel with the attempt, never in its place: the round is cut at once and their findings are applied to the members or the host while it certifies. Scar: 2026-11-03, two tracers caught an empty member (the fix was uncommitted) and an unrecorded half-activation from a usage probe (sp-uehe3b, sp-1m1lpo), after seven rounds had died on hypotheses.
