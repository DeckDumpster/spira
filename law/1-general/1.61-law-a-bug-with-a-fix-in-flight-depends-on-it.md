# §1.61 law-a-bug-with-a-fix-in-flight-depends-on-it

When a bug a person filed already has a fix in flight, neither close it nor leave it ready: make it depend on the fixing bead, so it leaves the queue and returns as the check on the fix. An incident a check filed and a resolver must close is the exception: link it relates-to, never blocked-by, force-close when resolving and log every failed close, because bd refuses to close a blocked bead. Scars: a P0 suite-timeout bug filed beside its open fix; 33 closed-not-landed incidents the resolver failed to close in silence (sp-tkspv).
