# §1.130 law-a-sweep-is-not-a-bead

A periodic check is not a unit of work. Run a sweep from its timer with no bead: file a bead only for what the sweep FINDS, identified by that finding, and record the pass itself in a ledger, which cannot be reopened. A bead's machinery — attempts, poison, closed-is-not-landed, repo: — is retry semantics for a deliverable that lands a commit, and a healthy sweep has none. Scar: of 76 sweep beads half were charged a failed attempt and eleven were poisoned for succeeding, one of them re-running a sweep that had just caught a real gate violation.
