# §1.182 law-config-through-the-cli-only

Nothing in the system knows where configuration lives or how it is stored. Every read and write of spira.toml goes through the spira-config CLI, or its library in Rust: never a path lookup, a TOML parser, a sed or a hand edit. It is typed, validated and atomic there, and nowhere else. A second reader drifts and a second writer corrupts. Scar: four parsers, a duplicate repo-map and one unvalidated edit made spira.toml unparseable for two minutes.
