# §1.46 law-only-spira-lc-talks-to-the-stores

Nothing but the store server runs bd, bd-embedded, bdq or dolt, or opens a SQL connection to the store: no crate, script, hook, test fixture, persona or session, under any path. Every read and write of bead content or lifecycle state goes through spira-lc; a missing operation becomes a new spira-lc verb, never a side door. The callers that remain are a list that only shrinks, and a new one is refused at the gate.
