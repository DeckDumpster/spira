# §1.318 law-regenerate-derived-summaries

Rewrite a derived document — a one-pager, crib sheet, exec summary or dashboard view — from scratch on every update rather than editing it in place as new findings land. The long source document accumulates corrections and reversals, and a patched summary silently retains superseded framing, which is the exact failure that made the source stale in the first place. Keep the long document as the record of workings and provenance, treat the short one as pure output, and note the regeneration rule inside the short document so a future session does not patch it by reflex.
