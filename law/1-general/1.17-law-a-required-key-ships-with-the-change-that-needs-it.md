# §1.17 law-a-required-key-ships-with-the-change-that-needs-it

A change that makes a configuration key required ships the key in the same change: its spira/conf.d file, conf.key-history line and config-delta.toml entry, so activating the release adds it to the live config. Nobody adds a required key to a live spira.toml by hand. Scar: sp-ngokxf made four keys required undeclared, and its builder asked the operator to hand-edit the live config (sp-m2dtii).
