# §1.30 law-a-measurement-ends-when-its-verdict-settles

A measurement runs until its verdict is settled, not for a round-number duration. State the limit and the confidence it must reach, report progress as it runs, and stop as soon as the count settles a pass or a fail; the only floor is the window the slowest periodic disturbance needs to appear. A fixed run that cannot fail early spends its whole length on a result already known. Scar: the store load gate ran a silent 30 minutes per run, so two runs contaminated on 2026-11-02 burned an hour before anyone saw they had failed (sp-90enop).
