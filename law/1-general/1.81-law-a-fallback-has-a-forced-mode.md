# §1.81 law-a-fallback-has-a-forced-mode

Every automatic fallback ships a no-fallback mode and a cheap check of its primary path, wired to a watcher, so a broken primary is red within minutes instead of hidden behind the fallback that absorbs it. Scar: round-vm fell back to the local hypervisor whenever its EC2 launch failed, so two launch defects in one evening looked like working rounds.
