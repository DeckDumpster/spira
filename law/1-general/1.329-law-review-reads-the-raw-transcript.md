# §1.329 law-review-reads-the-raw-transcript

A review of a session's own conduct, and any agent briefed to reconstruct what a session did or built, reads the raw transcript (the JSONL of user messages, assistant text, tool calls and tool results), never the session's compaction summaries or its own reports; where a summary and the raw events disagree the raw events win, and a summary is never cited as evidence. A session reviewing itself from its own summary grades the account it chose to keep. Scar: on 2026-10-11 the Concierge's review agent was first briefed from the Concierge's summary of the day it was reviewing.
