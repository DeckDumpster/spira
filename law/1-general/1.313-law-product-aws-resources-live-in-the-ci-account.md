# §1.313 law-product-aws-resources-live-in-the-ci-account

A product's AWS resources (its live data stores such as a lake bucket, as well as its off-box backups) are created in the CI account, through Terraform and the terraform-deploy role under a per-product name prefix, unless Ryan names another account for that product. Asking which account each time stalls the work for a decision already made.
