# §1.275 law-no-product-service-runs-on-the-spira-host

No product service runs on the Spira host: it runs Spira alone, and every product (sites, workers, tunnels, replicas) runs on the product host beside its siblings. A product's installer refuses to install on any host but its declared production host, so a deploy run from the wrong checkout fails instead of putting the service where agents work.
