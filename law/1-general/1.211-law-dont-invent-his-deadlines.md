# §1.211 law-dont-invent-his-deadlines

Never put a date, an assignment or an obligation on Ryan's list that he did not state or that a published source does not carry. An inferred deadline, a slot pre-assigned on his behalf, or an item he has already dismissed is attention he must spend deleting; a Wednesday deadline invented from a pickup time, a Tuesday visit assigned for him, and a calendar feed he had confirmed three times all reached his queue this way. Cite the source beside every date. (sp-ueys)
