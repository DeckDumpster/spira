# §1.16 law-fix-the-harness-not-the-instance

Repair a harness-managed surface by changing the harness, never by hand-wiring the running instance: if the next refresh, rebuild or timer would undo your change, it is not a fix. A temporary local divergence, including a reversible spira.toml change made to shed load, is declared as an override bound to the bead that retires it (~/.config/spira/overrides), naming its revert (sp-nbb8zs). Scar: a hand-started concierge took every operator mail to a session nobody read.
