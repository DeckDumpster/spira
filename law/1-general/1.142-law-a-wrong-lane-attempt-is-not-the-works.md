# §1.142 law-a-wrong-lane-attempt-is-not-the-works

An attempt spent in the wrong lane is not the work's fault: before poisoning or escalating a bead, check its labels put it in the lane that can land it; when builder work carries incident or alarm labels, relabel it to the builder and unpoison it without asking, naming the relabel as the cause. Scar: three round-duty poison asks in one evening, all answered by the same relabel.
