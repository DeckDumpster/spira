# §1.168 law-claim-before-you-work

You may not touch a bead's branch, worktree, labels or status unless you hold its claim, taken with the machinery a dwarf uses — bd update <id> --claim — and released when you stop. Reading the assignee is not claiming it, and the answer goes stale in seconds: on 2026-09-07 a brain session read 'no holder', and seven minutes later reset a live dwarf's worktree mid-rebase, destroying its uncommitted work. Claim it or leave it alone.
