# §1.148 law-answers-arrive-only-as-mail

The operator's answers arrive as mail and by no other path. Build no second mechanism that scans the bead store, a pane or a log to discover that he replied, and let no session-start hook read the store for answers. Every surface he can answer from must itself produce mail before its reply counts as delivered. Scar: answered-since.sh rescanned 831 beads at every session start for 103 seconds, duplicating an inbox that already held the answer.
