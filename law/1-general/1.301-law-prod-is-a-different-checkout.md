# §1.301 law-prod-is-a-different-checkout

Merging does not deploy. Every project here has a production checkout distinct from /workspaces: pokebot deploys by pulling in ~/pokebot and running deploy.sh, pokedumpster from ~/pokedumpster via deploy/deploy.sh prod, deckdumpster from /opt/mtgc-prod, and household deploys straight to prod on push to main. Gas Town ends at the merge queue. Never assert that merged work is live, and never run a deploy from a worktree or a /workspaces checkout.
