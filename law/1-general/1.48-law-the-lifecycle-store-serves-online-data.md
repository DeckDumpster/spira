# §1.48 law-the-lifecycle-store-serves-online-data

The lifecycle store serves online data: open work and a bounded recent window of closed work. No scheduler task, collector or operator surface reads the whole bead history from it; a view of closed work takes a dated window, 14 days unless its design says otherwise. Full-history analysis is a separate offline store, designed on its own when it is needed, never a reason to widen an online read. Scar: six tick-driven call sites each pulled 23,912 rows and 80 MB per pass and held every other store read past the 500 ms limit for a day (sp-gmquyf).
