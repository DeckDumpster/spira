# §1.286 law-only-the-operator-closes-his-ask

Only Ryan's answer closes an ask labelled needs-ryan. When he gives that answer anywhere (pane, mail, chat), the session that executed it closes the ask through the public tools (resolve or reply), quoting his words and saying what was done, so the close is auditable later. A session that has no answer from him acts on the escalated default at once and leaves the ask open for his ratification. What is not his class never carries the label (law-escalate-decisions-not-problems).
