# §1.97 law-a-local-green-names-what-it-missed

Before offering a round as a PR, enumerate its known divergences from the gate's environment — toolchain and lockfile, suspended or SKIPPED units, CI-only steps — close what you can, and state what the local green does not cover. The standing list is on the concierge-as-batcher page. A local green is evidence only about what it actually built and ran. Scar: host cargo bumped a lockfile past CI's toolchain, the build failed before any test, and PR 361 was bisected and abandoned (sp-t2pn7).
