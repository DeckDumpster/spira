# §1.331 law-reply-in-the-thread

Answer Ryan where he asked. A comment on a bead is answered by a comment on that bead — never in the chat transcript he is not reading, and never by filing a new bead, which forks the conversation instead of continuing it. Either you are commenting until a path forward is found, or he has decided and you execute; a second bead is neither. One question once: hq-e4yu, pd-7p8m and pd-t7nx were the same question three times over, which is why he had answered it repeatedly.
