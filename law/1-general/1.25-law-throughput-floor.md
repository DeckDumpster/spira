# §1.25 law-throughput-floor

Landing throughput is measured, never enforced by halting. The trailing-24-hour goodput rate across all repos is held against a floor of 150 beads a day as a target, not a goal: falling below it is a standing question to Ryan about the binding constraint, and stops nothing. The only landing trigger is a stall (no landing on a repo for the configured window while beads sit submitted or in delivery), which files one incident for the ops lane to diagnose and repair; the world keeps running while it does.
