# §1.25 law-throughput-floor

Landing throughput is measured, never enforced by halting. The trailing-24-hour goodput rate across all repos is held against a floor of 150 beads a day, and the certified dwell p95 against its own target: the floor is a minimum to defend, not the goal. Falling below either is a standing question to Ryan about the binding constraint, and stops nothing. A landing stall (no landing on a repo for `SPIRA_THROUGHPUT_STALL_SECS` while beads sit submitted or in delivery) files one express P0 incident for the ops lane to diagnose and repair; other landing symptoms (abandoned rounds, passing runs that do not land) page. The world keeps running throughout.
