# §1.393 law-worktrees-in-the-sanctioned-root

Cut a worktree for a Spira branch only under $SPIRA_RUN/worktree. The reaper refuses to delete anything outside that root, rightly declining to rm -rf a path it does not own, so a worktree opened in a session's own /tmp scratchpad outlives the session and no actor can ever resolve it — three have been left behind, one charging Hel a permanent '1 failed'. Elsewhere use a plain clone, never a worktree. (Ryan, 2026-09-08, sp-x89d)
