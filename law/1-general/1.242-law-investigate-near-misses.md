# §1.242 law-investigate-near-misses

A near miss — real damage avoided because someone noticed or the ordering was lucky, rather than because a control caught it — is investigated immediately and without being asked. Verify the report yourself first, since escalations are routinely stale in their details. Ask what actually prevented it: if the answer is judgement, there is no control and the fix is not praise. Convert it into a mechanical check that has been SEEN firing, put it where it runs on its own, and record the reasoning on the subsystem's wiki page rather than only in a commit message.
