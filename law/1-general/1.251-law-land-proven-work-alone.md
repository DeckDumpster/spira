# §1.251 law-land-proven-work-alone

Work whose verification already passed is landed on that evidence, alone, before anything new is batched with it: the batch composer never re-tests a proven member and never mixes it with untested members, because a new member's red then holds back work that was already done. Scar: a green round lost its certificate, was re-cut with three new members, and went red on theirs.
