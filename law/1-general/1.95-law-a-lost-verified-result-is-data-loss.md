# §1.95 law-a-lost-verified-result-is-data-loss

A completed verification that the system then discards or cannot record (a green certification, a passed gate, a finished acceptance) is a P0 data-loss incident, not a retry cost: the round or bead is landed from the existing evidence or the defect that lost it is fixed first, it pages as P0 until resolved, and re-running the verification is never reported as costless. Scar: a green round's certification was lost to a reply timeout, the round sat an hour paged as red, and the only exit was redoing the pass.
