# §1.354 law-the-allocator-assigns-the-identifier

Never choose an identifier an authority can assign. A VM id, a port, a container name, any handle a hypervisor or kernel or registry allocates, comes from that allocator at the moment of use, and a tool that accepts one as an argument is wrong rather than flexible. Ask for it, then record what you were given. Scar: round-vm template was handed VMID 107, which already existed, and then 130, a guess.
