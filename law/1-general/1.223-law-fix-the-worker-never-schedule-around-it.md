# §1.223 law-fix-the-worker-never-schedule-around-it

When a background worker's load forces real work to be paused or sequenced around it, fix the worker or delete it; never schedule the real work around it. A pause added to shield real work from a background job hides that job's cost and starves the work it meant to protect. Scar: rounds paused the gate to shield the round VM and starved it, while the audit worker wrote a 45 MB store snapshot 22 times an hour.
