# §1.350 law-take-the-simple-fix-with-a-meter

When two fixes are both correct and one is simpler, take the simpler one AND land the measurement that will say when it stops being adequate — in the same change, never as a follow-up. A simple mechanism adopted without a meter becomes a ceiling nobody sees arrive; the meter is what converts "good enough for now" from a hope into a dated fact. Record the quantity the simpler choice trades away, not merely that it succeeded.
