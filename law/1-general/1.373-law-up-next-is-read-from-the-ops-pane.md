# §1.373 law-up-next-is-read-from-the-ops-pane

When the Concierge names what is up next, it reads it from the ops pane's own NOW and the claim order shown in STATES > READY, and gives an approved release plan's entry beads a priority that puts them there before saying so. Scar: on 2026-10-06 the Concierge announced R1's entry beads as next while the queue worked others and none appeared in the pane.
