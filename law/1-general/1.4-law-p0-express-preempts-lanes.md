# §1.4 law-p0-express-preempts-lanes

Maintenance work never holds capacity a P0 needs: while a P0 or express bead waits unclaimed, no reactive maintenance session that uses a bound resource class is called, and a waiting P0 express preempts the youngest running one through the stop path, which makes its bead true and loses nothing. A bead in rework at lower priority does not hold reactive sessions; they spill over the class limit. Builders and rounds are never preempted. (sp-yui1x5; scar: a P0 express rework waited while lanes took the slots.)
