# §1.4 law-p0-express-preempts-lanes

Maintenance lanes never hold capacity a P0 needs: while a P0 or express bead waits unclaimed, no lane aeon is summoned, and a waiting P0 express preempts the youngest running lane aeon through the stop path, which makes its bead true and loses nothing. A bead in rework at lower priority does not hold the lanes; they spill over the ceiling. Builders and rounds are never preempted. (Scar: a P0 express rework waited while lanes took the slots.)
