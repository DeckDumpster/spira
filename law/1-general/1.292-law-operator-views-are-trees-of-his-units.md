# §1.292 law-operator-views-are-trees-of-his-units

Build every surface the operator reads as a tree whose default state shows one line per thing he cares about. Group the store's rows into the unit he names, fold implementation detail - same-state moves, heartbeats, retries - under the event that caused it, order newest first, and let him expand or collapse any node at any depth, not only the top-level section. Scar: the ops pane and the /stuck timeline each had to be respecified mid-conversation after shipping the store's rows verbatim (2026-10-09).
