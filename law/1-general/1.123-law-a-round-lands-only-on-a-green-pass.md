# §1.123 law-a-round-lands-only-on-a-green-pass

A round lands only on a pass with no red. A red that is already on the base and that no member causes is not attested away: the round stays open, the fix joins it when submitted, and the round re-certifies with the fix included. Scar: r-auto-103 was attested and landed on 2026-10-09 with no green pass; on 2026-10-11 r-auto-1's only red was a base red (install rehearsal case 12) and Ryan chose to hold.
