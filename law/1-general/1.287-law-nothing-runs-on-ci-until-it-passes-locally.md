# §1.287 law-nothing-runs-on-ci-until-it-passes-locally

Nothing runs on CI until it has passed locally on the same commit: a publish PR only after a full-suite local round of that commit is green, a release cut only after acceptance-local.sh phases A-D (with --predecessor) are green on that commit. CI is confirmation, never discovery. A local runner that cannot pass is the first defect to fix, never a reason to go to CI instead. Scar: on 2026-10-04 local acceptance was broken, the Concierge routed around it, and about eight forty-five-minute CI round trips each surfaced one defect a local run finds in minutes.
