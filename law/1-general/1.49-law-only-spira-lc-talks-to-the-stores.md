# §1.49 law-only-spira-lc-talks-to-the-stores

Nothing talks to dolt or beads directly. spira-lc is the one door onto the stores: every read of bead content or lifecycle state goes through the store server, on a pooled connection. bead.sh and bdq are wrappers for content writes only. No crate, script, handler or test fixture outside the store server forks bd or bdq for a read, runs dolt, or opens a SQL connection to the dolt server. A missing operation becomes a new spira-lc verb, never a side door. A design that names a store implementation means the door, not the database. Scars: sp-0etw8a landed a DoltCli journal in the runtime crate and sp-pzah5b built on it (2026-10-28); 2,935 bdq read forks in six hours, 340 ms each against 30 ms through the door (sp-bt2gyz, 2026-11-11).
