# §1.324 law-reading-his-accounts-is-a-permissions-ask

Work that gives an automation read access to the operator's personal accounts (order history, mailbox, calendar, statements, any record of what he did outside the system) is escalated as a permissions ask and built only on his answer, and what such a read finds may never relax a limit the automation enforces on itself. (sp-h8z3p1; scar: pokebot's order reconciler read his Amazon order history unannounced for ten weeks, then let three orders he cancelled by hand free its purchase cap into six buys against three.)
