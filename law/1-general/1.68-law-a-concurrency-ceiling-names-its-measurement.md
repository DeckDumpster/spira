# §1.68 law-a-concurrency-ceiling-names-its-measurement

A concurrency ceiling (live aeons, gate slots, certify width, round size) is set only with the measurement that justifies it and the condition that retires it, recorded beside the value; when that condition is met the ceiling is re-measured, never left standing on a reason that has passed. Scar: the live-aeon ceiling was hand-set 8, then 6, then 4, and each number outlived the condition that justified it.
