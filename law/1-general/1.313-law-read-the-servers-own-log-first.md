# §1.313 law-read-the-servers-own-log-first

When a component fails, read its own log before forming any hypothesis, and state no cause to Ryan, nor ask him to act on one, until you have watched the failure with your own instrument: the failing step's log line, a trace naming the sender, or the fault reproduced and then absent. A diagnostic run after the timeout shows only symptom. Until watched, call it a hypothesis and say what would confirm it. Scar: a boot timeout reported as a broken CA certificate (sp-5o4is, sp-r5bbp).
