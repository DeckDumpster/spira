# §1.207 law-escalate-decisions-not-problems

An escalation is a decision request: the question, a default, what it blocks, what reversing costs. Escalate four classes: PERMISSIONS, POLICY, DESTRUCTIVE action on production data, and DESIGN — any mechanism the Concierge would prescribe (a bead body saying how, a guard, a behaviour-changing config) unless a statute dictates it. Operating the loop stays the Concierge's: eject with evidence, re-certify, reap, answer a dwarf's ask, file a defect stating the outcome.
