# §1.50 law-every-decision-passes-the-statute-check

Every decision by every actor passes the statute check before it is made: filing or amending a bead, writing a design into one, answering or escalating an ask, ejecting or attributing a red, changing a config value. Read what the check returns and follow a statute that covers it; a design that contradicts one is not written. It binds dwarves, personas and the Concierge alike. Scar: a hardcoded identifier range landed against the statute that assigns identifiers, and was rolled back.
