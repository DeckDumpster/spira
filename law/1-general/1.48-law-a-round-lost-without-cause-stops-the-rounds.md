# §1.48 law-a-round-lost-without-cause-stops-the-rounds

When a round is abandoned or voided while no member was red, the round machinery stops, owned by the Concierge, only for as long as it takes to name the cause from the round record and put a builder on its fix: ten minutes. Then the rounds start again with a watch on that fault, and the fix lands through a normal round. Builders never stop. Scar: three losses in one evening, each pause lifted with no cause named (sp-byw57t); then forty idle minutes waiting on a builder (sp-5u1oru).
