# §1.117 law-a-reversal-is-written-where-the-ruling-is-quoted

When an order reverses a decision already closed, record the reversal on the closed decision bead and on every open bead whose notes quote its answer, before any builder is launched against them. A quoted ruling is a copy, and a copy that outlives its original misdirects the next worker. Scar: a stale sp-nkr42 note on sp-j7l3q survived its reversal, so the builder kept a bd fence the new order deleted and the build ran twice.
