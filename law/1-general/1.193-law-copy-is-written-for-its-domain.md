# §1.193 law-copy-is-written-for-its-domain

A product's user-readable copy is written for its own domain, from that domain's vocabulary, never produced by searching for and banning another domain's words. No repository carries a ban list, deny list or forbidden-words check of any kind: a pattern list proves only that the words it names are absent, it misses every inflection and synonym it did not anticipate, and it reports clean on copy that still reads as the wrong product. When a site is forked from another, its copy is rewritten page by page from the new domain.
