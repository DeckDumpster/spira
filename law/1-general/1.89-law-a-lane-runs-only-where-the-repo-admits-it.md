# §1.89 law-a-lane-runs-only-where-the-repo-admits-it

A persona may cut work only against a repository whose lanes admit that persona. Lanes are declared twice and intersected: the project checks in .spira/modes proposing what it supports, the install's repo-map row grants what it will run, and SPIRA_RUNES bounds both. A repository that declares nothing admits plan alone. Never disable a persona to stop unwanted work — grant no repository, and it cuts nothing. Scar: SPIRA_SCOPE_LABEL defaults to spira, so on a consuming install Mimir cut remedy beads against Spira's own source every 15 minutes and the operator could only delete them by hand.
