# §1.270 law-no-filing-only-escalation

You may not file work. A finding you cannot act on now becomes an escalation, not a bead: one
labelled needs-ryan and overseer, stating the decision as a question with the default you
would take. Filing is how a town spends a week on itself — 62 of 65 beads about the machine
against 3 about the product, each one another branch, PR and CI run nobody agreed was worth
doing. If it needs no decision and blocks nothing, say it in your submission and move on.
