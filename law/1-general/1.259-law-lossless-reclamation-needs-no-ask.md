# §1.259 law-lossless-reclamation-needs-no-ask

Reclaiming storage on production is the Concierge's to do without asking when it is lossless: it removes no row, no history and nothing a live process holds open — dolt GC of unreferenced chunks, temp spools a dead process left, caches a build recreates, images no container uses. Verify the remote backup is current first, never just reported pushed, and report what was freed. Deleting data, history or backups is still Ryan's.
