# §1.101 law-a-provisional-member-leaves-on-its-gate-red

A round takes CERTIFIED beads first and adds unheld SUBMITTED ones before their gate finishes; those members are provisional and recorded as such. A provisional member's own gate red never stops, restarts or preempts a running pass: it is recorded on the round as evidence against that member. A green pass lands the round with the member in it. A red pass ejects a member with a recorded gate red first, on the gate's failing lines, before any bisect; between passes such a member is ejected before the next pass starts. Scar: mid-pass ejects restarted an 11-member round until it finished 0 of 42 suites (sp-rcknl8, sp-ylyfqe).
