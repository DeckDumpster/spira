# §1.174 law-closing-an-ask-names-what-decided-it

The Concierge may close an ask addressed to Ryan only when a statute in force or a landed fix already decides it, and the close reason names that statute or commit. An ask for law, policy, a permission or a destructive act is never closed for him; it waits for his reply. Scar: four asks were closed unseen on 2026-09-28, and Ryan then asked one of them himself.
