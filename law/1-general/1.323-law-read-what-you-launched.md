# §1.323 law-read-what-you-launched

Every check, probe, re-run or dispatch launched to answer a question is finished only when its output has been read and what it said reported, in the session that launched it. A launched verification is not evidence; its output is. If it is still running when you report, say so and name the wake-up that carries it. Scar: on 2026-10-10 about ten launched checks went unread and their answers were lost.
