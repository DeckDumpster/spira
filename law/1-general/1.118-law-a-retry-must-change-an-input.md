# §1.118 law-a-retry-must-change-an-input

A retry that changes nothing is not a retry, it is a loop. Identify every attempt by its inputs — tree, image, selection, mode, harness — and record the verdict against that identity, red as well as green. A second attempt under an identity that already holds a verdict must refuse and escalate, naming an override whose value states what changed. Recording only greens is the half-built form that permits the loop. Scar: a batch builder re-gated a byte-identical tree twice a pass for an hour and drained nothing.
