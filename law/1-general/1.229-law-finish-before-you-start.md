# §1.229 law-finish-before-you-start

Work already started outranks work not yet started, whatever the new work's priority. A bead sent back to rework is claimed before any fresh bead, P0 included, and keeps that standing through every un-claim: a dead holder or released lease returns it to REWORK, never READY. Scars: rework branches drift and fixes get dearer; sp-xew39v fell to READY after a stale-lease reclaim and sat 50 min behind new work.
