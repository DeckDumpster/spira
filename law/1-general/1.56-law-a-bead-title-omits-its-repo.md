# §1.56 law-a-bead-title-omits-its-repo

Every bead is filed with its repository as a required, explicit field of the filing facade, on every filing path, with no default, and every surface that renders a bead shows the repository from that field. A title may name its repository; that is never an error. Scar: titles carried the repo because the field was not obvious, and a title-refusal added to fix it broke a harness filing path.
