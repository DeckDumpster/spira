# §1.203 law-deploy-the-tested-artifacts

Deploy the artifacts the passing run tested, never a rebuild. A round's corpus builds its binaries from the exact tree it certifies; landing installs those, by copy-then-rename, and refuses when they are missing rather than building fresh. A rebuild is a different artifact from the one that passed, and a source move without binaries runs new scripts against old programs. Scar: production ran round 97's scripts against a spira-config built before round 97's schema.
