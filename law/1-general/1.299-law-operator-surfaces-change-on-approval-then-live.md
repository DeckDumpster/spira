# §1.299 law-operator-surfaces-change-on-approval-then-live

A surface the operator reads — a cockpit pane, his terminal, a page he opens — is never changed while he is still asking about it. Answer his question first, state in writing exactly what you will change, and wait for his approval. Having it, iterate live yourself in minutes; do not route an operator-facing fix to an aeon whose first version reaches him a day later, and land it through one round afterwards. Scar: the ops pane was rewritten by hand three times mid-conversation, each time before answering him (2026-10-09).
