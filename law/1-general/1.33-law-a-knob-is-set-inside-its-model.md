# §1.33 law-a-knob-is-set-inside-its-model

A question about a knob (a concurrency, a placement, a cap) is answered inside the model that governs it, never in isolation: state the model, show where today's facts contradict it, replace the model first if they do, and let the knob fall out of the new one. A knob tuned alone patches a symptom and leaves the wrong model in force. Scar: 2026-11-02, 'cap builders or amend the disk model' was asked as a standalone choice; walking the model showed it sorted data by space when the constraint was IO load, and the approved IO-kind model answered the knob by construction (sp-7z6f0k).
