# §1.360 law-test-selection-and-plan-are-one-source

Test selection, the test plan and its coverage documentation derive from one source, so no merge can make one disagree with another. The selector reads the mapping the plan is generated from; coverage.json and COVERAGE.md are generated from it at the point of use, never hand-committed as independent state that a merge can leave stale.
