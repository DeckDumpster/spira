# §1.170 law-close-on-merge-not-submit

Close a bead when its work has LANDED, never when it was submitted. Verify with git merge-base --is-ancestor against the target branch, not by the merge request existing. Four pokedumpster beads were closed over open, RED pull requests; two were failing a real gate, and because the board said done those failures had no owner and nobody was going to fix them. gt orphans is the hunt and settings/orphan-watch.sh now runs it every six hours.
