# §1.134 law-a-target-closes-only-met-or-contradicted

A bead that names a target closes only when the target is met, shown by the measurement it names, or contradicted, shown by evidence that it cannot be met as stated. An aeon may give up, but its bead may not: before it stops short it files the follow-on bead that carries the remaining distance, or escalates the target as a decision, and the close names which. Scar: the sim-world bead targeted under 10 s, closed at about 37 s with 'the world is still above 10 s', and nothing was filed.
