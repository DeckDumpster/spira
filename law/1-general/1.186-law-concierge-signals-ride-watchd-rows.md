# §1.186 law-concierge-signals-ride-watchd-rows

Any signal the Concierge must not miss is written by a watchd row or mail-deliver to the durable inbox (watchd/concierge-inbox.log), never typed into the pane: keystrokes land inside Ryan's half-written messages. The session tails the inbox with a triage Monitor (inbox-triage.sh) that drops echoes and duplicates and passes what needs action. The inbox-keeper row is the only thing that may type, a single re-arm line when no monitor has run for 10 minutes, held until the input box is empty.
