# §1.327 law-reversibility-outranks-coverage

Reversibility outranks test coverage. Prefer a change ops can ship incrementally and undo by reverting it and rolling forward, over one that is merely well tested — a cheap, fast gate is worth having and is not the goal. Backward-incompatible changes are not to be avoided, but one that cannot be undone by a revert is Ryan's decision to make, escalated before it is built rather than after.
