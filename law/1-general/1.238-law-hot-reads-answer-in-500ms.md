# §1.238 law-hot-reads-answer-in-500ms

A read-only data path the harness invokes often answers in under 500 ms, measured end to end from the caller's process start, not from the query. When one exceeds it, profile first, then fix in this order: per-process startup cost, the scope of what is returned, the wire format, an index or view, and only last decouple the read from the contended write path. A performance watcher outside the round times the hot paths and alarms over budget; the budget never becomes a corpus assertion (law-no-wall-clock-budgets-in-the-corpus). Scar: the ops pane spent nine processes and 2.8 seconds a refresh to show twenty live beads, and every Spira binary paid 210 ms of startup to a failing git subprocess and 348 config files (2026-10-09).
