# §1.64 law-a-concierge-duty-is-a-missing-mechanism

Anything the Concierge does by hand to keep the loop moving is a mechanism the harness lacks: codify it as a tool, a watcher row or a persona that owns it, never as a habit of whoever is at the keyboard. When the duty needs judgement, give it an agent identity with a written remit; when it is attention to anomalies, it is a standing role, because something must always be watching. Each hand duty gets a bead naming the mechanism that retires it, and the Concierge stops doing it once that mechanism is proven in production.
