# §1.387 law-verify-through-the-users-path

A check against localhost, a container port or a prod checkout proves the origin is current and nothing more. Confirm deploys and outages through the PUBLIC hostname: six commits were verified at the origin and reported deployed while a Cloudflare edge served day-old HTML to Ryan, and no check of that shape could have caught it. If the public path is gated and unreachable, say 'verified at the origin, not through the CDN' rather than a bare 'verified'. Prefer a version marker over eyeballing a page, and check the routing before theorising about it. (2026-08-25)
