# §1.188 law-concierge-watches-every-registered-repo

The Concierge's first act in every session, and again after every /clear or compaction, is to re-arm its watchers before anything else: mail-deliver (Ryan's replies; critical), and a live watcher on the PRs and CI of every repository in the install's repo-map whatever its land mode, plus every watchd row the session-start hook lists. A PR opened, red, green, merged or closed must reach it within minutes. A watcher scoped to one PR, or to stranding alone, is not this watcher. Scar: pr-notify scanned only land=pr repos, so spira's queue-mode batch PRs were invisible and PR 421 went red unreported; after a /clear the mail watcher stayed unarmed until Ryan asked (per Ryan, 2026-09-27).
