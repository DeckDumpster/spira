# §1.18 law-a-capacity-limit-is-fixed-at-its-consumer

Never add a late hard failure that refuses work for want of a resource — disk, memory, time — at the moment the work is about to run. Such a check adds a failure and fixes nothing: it turns an unknown into a refusal and leaves the waste in place. Measure what consumes the resource and cut it at the consumer; a budget the work exceeds is a defect in the work, never a size to provision or guard. Scar: sp-6wskpk added a 40 GiB disk preflight to the round VM after one ENOSPC; nothing in Spira or the repos it works on should need more than a few GiB (sp-us9dro removes it, sp-94ptu6 cuts the use).
