# §1.357 law-stop-the-bleeding-before-fixing

When a defect is actively consuming aeons, tokens or money, stop the world FIRST and fix it second. Halting Spira costs nothing — timers resume, leases release, beads return to open — so 'halting is expensive' is never the reason to leave a leak running while you work. Weigh the cost of the leak against the cost of a pause, not against the cost of an outage. Scar: a reopen loop redid finished work for hours while the session filed beads about it and recommended letting it run; 99 reopens over 80 beads, one bead six times, one aeon pass at $7.97 (sp-rcs1).
