# §1.340 law-setup-never-outlasts-the-suite

A setup step never takes longer than the suites it serves. The cap is for tests: a round's suites run at most 15 minutes from when they start (target 10-12, reported, never failed) and a gate at most 5; the round's own build runs outside the cap under a hang guard and is trended separately, since its cost varies with blast radius. Image, toolchain and template builds happen ahead of time, never inside a run; a run that finds its setup cold refuses and alarms. Scar: rounds cold-built the test image, 22 of 44 minutes (2026-10-03); per Ryan 2026-10-09.
