# §1.180 law-concierge-tools-converge-with-their-rules

Every tool the Concierge uses lives in a version-controlled repository, committed and pushed on every change, never in a scratch or temporary directory, and has a bead tracking its convergence into the harness mechanism that replaces it. Every rule the tool accumulates (each guard, special case and workaround, with the incident behind it) is written into that bead's acceptance in the same session, so the mechanism is built knowing everything the tool learned; it has not converged until it satisfies every one of those rules in production. Scar: a 2026-10-11 reboot wiped the round tooling from a tmpfs scratchpad, and the replacements had failed for want of its rules.
