# §1.61 law-a-catchup-targets-a-fixed-point

A bead never aims at a moving target. Any backfill, catch-up, migration, re-tag or 'bring everything into line' work names a point-in-time target in its spec: a commit id, snapshot or timestamp. It is done when that target is met, and whatever lands afterwards belongs to a follow-up bead or to the enforcement it introduces. A check that would fail on later arrivals lands report-only first and turns enforcing in a separate bead.
