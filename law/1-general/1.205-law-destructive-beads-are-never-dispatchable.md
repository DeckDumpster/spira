# §1.205 law-destructive-beads-are-never-dispatchable

A bead whose procedure stops, restarts or reinstalls the harness — world.sh stop, systemd/install.sh, a schema migration, anything needing the world stopped — must be DEFERRED at the moment it is filed. The harness dispatches it, so a worker destroys its own executor and every dwarf in flight; sp-6ylz killed three dwarves and itself four times. Defer is the fence. Never label the work bead needs-ryan: that makes it an ask, and answering an ask closes it — two work beads read CLOSED on a panel reply, satisfying the alignment check that was blocked on them. Put the question on its own ask that BLOCKS the work. (sp-6ylz, sp-62el)
