# §1.231 law-guard-binds-the-caller

A guard must bind the actor who broke the rule, never the path or command the call travelled through. Before wrapping anything, grep every caller — including the tool's own plumbing, and the systemd unit templates: a unit's ExecStart is a caller the source never names, so grepping the source alone reports a function as having one door when it has two. The mail shim has now cut two innocent callers: the watchers' escalation channel to Ryan, and gt mq submit's notify step, which silently stranded four polecats holding finished work. Identify the caller from argv[0] of the parent via /proc, never a command-line pattern — that matches your own arguments.
