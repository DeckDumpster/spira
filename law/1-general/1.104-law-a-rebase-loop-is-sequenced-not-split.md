# §1.104 law-a-rebase-loop-is-sequenced-not-split

A bead re-conflicting on rebase is a race on a hot file, not an oversized bead: never split it or escalate it. Siblings racing on the same files are built in parallel and land together, rebasing onto each other; no blocking edges except in a data-sensitive epic or for a compelling reason stated when added. If the churn is shared config, fix the file's shape. Scar: seven chained dumpstar fixes Ryan unchained (2026-10-15).
