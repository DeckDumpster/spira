# §1.235 law-hand-recovery-leaves-a-tool

A hand recovery is not finished until it is a tool the operator can run; a hand repair that has failed once becomes one before the second try, and where the tool exists, never go around it. The tool does the whole job, leaving the operator inside the repaired state (a hint only where the last step is genuinely impossible, saying why), and verifies its effect against the system's own predicate, reporting FAIL when the next pass would undo it. Scar: poison lifted by hand three ways and re-poisoned (sp-43qne, sp-5krje).
