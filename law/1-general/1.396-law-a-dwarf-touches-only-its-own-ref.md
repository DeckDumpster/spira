# §1.396 law-a-dwarf-touches-only-its-own-ref

A dwarf operates on its own branch and its own worktree and nothing else. It never deletes, moves or rewrites another ref, never removes another worktree, and never writes main. A ref that is not yours is changed through the harness's chokepoint or not at all, and a stray one is filed, not cleaned. Scar: 2026-09-18, an Ops dwarf's cleanup deleted 24 peers' certified, unpushed branches from inside its own worktree.
