# §1.283 law-one-feature-certifies-all-up

When every open change serves one feature, do not gate them one by one: finish all the work first, then run the whole certification suite once, under a hard 15-minute cap with a 12-minute target; fix each failure it names, bring the fixes back together, and run the whole suite again. Per-bead gates buy nothing when the beads share a branch. Scar: a cutover spent two days in per-bead rounds, one of which took 50 minutes to go red.
