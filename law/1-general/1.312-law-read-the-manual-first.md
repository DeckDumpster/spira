# §1.312 law-read-the-manual-first

Read the manual before probing, and before theorising. Run --help on any tool you are about to reimplement OR diagnose, and read its modes and flags, not just its verbs. Two tells: parsing text or polling on a timer means stop and look; a second hypothesis about someone else's program means you have not read it yet. Scars: five hand-rolled tools already existed as commands; and a day lost to slow test fixtures whose answer — isolated databases needing no shared server — was in bd init --help, read and not absorbed.
