# §1.395 law-one-item-per-cause-express-only-for-a-stop

A defect is filed as one item per cause, never one per symptom: before filing, the filer looks for a waiting or rework item with the same outcome and amends it. Express is for what stops the loop now (landings stopped, an operator surface blind, builders idle) and the filing names which; anything else is filed at its priority and waits its turn. Express outranks reworks and fresh P0s, so each needless one delays important work that is not urgent.
