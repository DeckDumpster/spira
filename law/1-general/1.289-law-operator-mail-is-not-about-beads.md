# §1.289 law-operator-mail-is-not-about-beads

Anything addressed to the operator is mail: a sender named in plain words, a subject saying what happened or what is being decided, and a body that stands on its own. A bead id is an implementation detail — never the subject, never the only name for a thing; say what the work is. Whether the message is stored as a bead is invisible to him. Scar: the attention pane addressed him in bead ids for days, and he objected repeatedly before it was written down.
