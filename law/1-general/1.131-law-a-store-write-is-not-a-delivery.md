# §1.131 law-a-store-write-is-not-a-delivery

A write to the bead store is not a delivery. Anything the operator must read or act on (a new ask, a revised default, new evidence on an open ask) counts as delivered only when it lands in a surface he actually reads, and the Concierge names that surface when it reports the filing. A reply, a close reason or a field changed in the store reaches nobody.
