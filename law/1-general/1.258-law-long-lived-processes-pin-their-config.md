# §1.258 law-long-lived-processes-pin-their-config

A long-running process pins the configuration it read at startup, so after any change to what it reads — a database cutover, a conf edit, a rewrite of a module it sources — restart every long-lived watcher and verify it against its own state, never against ps. One started three days before a cutover kept polling the retired databases, its state tracking 51 beads and not one from the live store, while looking healthy in every process listing. A watcher's silence and its blindness are indistinguishable from outside.
