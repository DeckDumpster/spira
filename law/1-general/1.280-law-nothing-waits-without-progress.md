# §1.280 law-nothing-waits-without-progress

Nothing in Spira waits without progress for more than a few seconds unnoticed: every wait state has a heartbeat, a bound measured in seconds (minutes only for an external dependency in progress), and an automatic action its owning component takes when the bound passes — re-drive, eject, abandon, restart or release. Paging a person is the fallback when that action fails, never the first response.
