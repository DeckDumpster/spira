# §1.238 law-green-prs-merge-themselves

A passing PR must merge without a human. Enable repository auto-merge and arm it when you open the PR; a green PR that waits is not merely idle, it rots — master moves underneath it and manufactures conflicts that did not exist. Six sat overnight on pokedumpster with auto-merge disabled at the repo level, two of them green, and the wait alone turned them dirty. Never file a rebase you could do; the waiting caused it.
