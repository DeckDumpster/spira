# §1.107 law-a-refusal-names-its-exit

A check that blocks a legitimate operation must name, in its own refusal message, why it refused and the sanctioned way through: an input, a flag or a named procedure that records who claimed the precondition and on what evidence. A refusal with no exit leaves the caller stuck, or moving the thing by hand, which disarms a correct check silently. The exit must require the claim, never merely permit the bypass. Scar: advance-v1.yml refused with a bare exit 1 and named no way past (sp-d5pn0, per Ryan 2026-09-24).
