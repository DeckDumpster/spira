# §1.76 law-a-design-prices-what-it-rejects

A design that rejects an alternative states its cost in measured or explicitly estimated numbers — what it covers and misses, what must be installed and where, what config must be written, the ongoing cost — and never dismisses it with an adjective. An estimate is labelled as one. When the numbers favour the alternative, the design changes and says so. Scar: the locked-dependencies design v2 (2026-10-12) chose mise and waved off Nix as a large adoption cost; measured, nixpkgs already carried dolt 2.4.2, beads 1.3.1 and podman 5.8.8, and Nix covered the podman drift mise could not, so v3 reversed to Nix.
