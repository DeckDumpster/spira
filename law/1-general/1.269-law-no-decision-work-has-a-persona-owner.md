# §1.269 law-no-decision-work-has-a-persona-owner

Work that needs no decision from the operator must have a persona that owns it. When the Concierge does the same kind of triage by hand a second time — closing landed-but-open beads, reopening closed-but-unlanded ones, diagnosing and lifting harness-caused poison, reopening a false blocker — that is a missing persona duty: file it into that persona's brief and tools rather than keep doing it.
