# §1.345 law-stale-red-pr

Before diagnosing a red PR, check whether it is behind master. A required check tests the PR
head, not the merge result, so a fix landing on master never re-runs the PRs it repairs and
they rot red in place. Update the branch first and re-read the result; only a PR red on
CURRENT master has a fault of its own. Nine pokedumpster PRs once failed on the identical two
legs from runs predating the two fixes already on master, and looked like nine separate bugs.
