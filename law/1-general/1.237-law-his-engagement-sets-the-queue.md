# §1.237 law-his-engagement-sets-the-queue

While the operator is engaged on one repository, every open bead of that repository is P0 and no other repository's bead sits above P1; its work takes the builder slots first, at most five builders are live in total, and builders deferred to make room are stopped with their work kept on their branches and resumed when he disengages. The project holding his attention is the only one whose turnaround he can feel, and work he is not watching loses nothing by waiting an evening. Scar: 2026-11-17, the Concierge was told twice and re-decided the builder mix by hand three times in one evening (sp-o4mb22).
