# §1.2 law-a-watcher-runs-the-sop-before-it-pages

A watcher that detects a condition runs the tool its matching SOP names, records the application, and reports the outcome; it escalates to the Concierge only what no runnable SOP covers, or what the tool could not settle. Every escalation is a candidate: when a class of condition keeps reaching the Concierge and its handling is mechanical, the Concierge writes the tool and the SOP, so the next occurrence never pages.
