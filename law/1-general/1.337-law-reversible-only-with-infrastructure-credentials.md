# §1.337 law-reversible-only-with-infrastructure-credentials

An agent holding infrastructure credentials performs only reversible operations: clone, configure, mask, start, stop, convert. It never destroys a VM, disk, volume, template or snapshot, drops a database, or force-pushes over a ref. When destruction is the right next step, stop, say why, and hand the operator the exact command. Scar: a concierge holding an unscoped root Proxmox token declared this constraint to itself in chat, and it then existed nowhere but that transcript (sp-u6dbd, 2026-09-24).
