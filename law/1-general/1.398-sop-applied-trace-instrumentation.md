# §1.398 sop-applied-trace-instrumentation

MATCH: sop\.sh applied.*(hang|timed? ?out|13[0-9]s)|sop applied.*(slow|~130s)

CHECK: test -f spira/sop.sh; time sop applied <slug> --bead X --check pass --held yes (should take well under 5s)
FIX: If still slow on the Rust binary, time sop list and sop applied separately; look at ledger and bd memories latency. Do not blame dolt contention without a trace.
ESCALATE: If sop applied is still above 30s on the Rust binary, file a bead with timings.
