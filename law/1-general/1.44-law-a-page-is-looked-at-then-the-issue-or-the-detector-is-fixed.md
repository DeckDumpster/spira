# §1.44 law-a-page-is-looked-at-then-the-issue-or-the-detector-is-fixed

Every page is looked at when it arrives, by whoever receives it, whatever its class and however often that class has been false. Looking ends one of two ways: the issue it names is fixed, or the detector that raised it is fixed so it cannot raise that page falsely again. A reader never filters, mutes or skims a page class; a filter on pages is a defect in the reader. Scar: stall pages had been false earlier in the day, so the Concierge filtered them from its inbox reads, and a round the batcher opened and never drove sat dead for 26 minutes while three correct pages went unread (sp-pi2ojm).
