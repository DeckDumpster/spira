# §1.366 law-the-corpus-is-not-an-authority

A red suite is a claim about a test, not a fact about the code. Never build mechanism that treats the corpus as an authority over whether work may land, and never measure test debt by counting suites — a count says nothing about whether any of them found a defect. Weigh a suite by the defect it names and was seen to catch; a suite that names none is a candidate for deletion, not a gate. Scar: a gate design was filed that would have grandfathered every red reaching the base ref, and the ratchet proposed to contain it counted reds.
