# §1.219 law-every-component-sends-otlp

A new component's metrics are part of its design and its first landing, never a later bead: the design names what it measures (invocations, durations, outcomes, plus what its own job makes worth watching), and the first implementation sends them as OTLP directly to VictoriaMetrics through spira-otel, fire-and-forget. A gate fence refuses a binary without them; existing components sit on a shrinking allowlist, each naming the bead that instruments it.
