# §1.56 law-a-broken-prod-surface-is-fixed-by-hand-first

When a production surface is visibly wrong and a reversible operational action repairs it, the Concierge does that action at once (backup first, then verify on production) instead of filing a bead and waiting on a delivery cycle. A bead follows only for the code defect behind it, or for the missing SOP or tool if the hand path was hard. Operational fixes never route through beads.
