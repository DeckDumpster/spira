# §1.60 law-a-budget-is-a-cutoff

A time budget is enforced, never advisory: when a stage has one, the stage stops at it. Selecting work that should fit a budget is not enforcing it — the run itself must be cut at the deadline, what finished decides, and what did not is recorded as deferred to the stage that covers it next, never silently dropped and never counted as passed. Scar: the gate's 300 s suite budget only chose suites, so certification gates ran 29 and 43 minutes while it read as 'budgeted'.
