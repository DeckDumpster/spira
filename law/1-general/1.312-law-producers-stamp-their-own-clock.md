# §1.312 law-producers-stamp-their-own-clock

Every line a producer writes for a reader opens with the event's own UTC time in a fixed-width ISO8601 prefix, and every staleness or escalation clock reads that stamp rather than the moment a consumer first saw the line. A reader cannot recover a time the producer knew and dropped. Scar: a dead watcher wrote its whole backlog the instant it recovered, so the most overdue answers looked the newest and drew a fresh grace period.
