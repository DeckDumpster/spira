# §1.75 law-a-design-for-approval-reaches-his-phone

A design put to Ryan for approval is published where he can read and comment on it from his phone — a Claude Doc linked directly under the design page's title — and in the same act its approval ask is filed, naming the defaults, so the decision reaches him as mail. Scar: the R1 design waited in hunk until he asked; the Loom portal doc went out without its ask (2026-10-15).
