# §1.135 law-a-wait-names-what-it-waits-for

A wait or barrier names what it waits for — the beads, runs, processes or signal — and its duration is only a cap on that wait, never the condition. Nothing opens or proceeds because a clock ran out: a gate lifts on the event or by an explicit act, and at the cap the waiter acts on the named stragglers (gives up naming them, or slays them by name). Scar: on 2026-10-15 world drain was a 30-minute window; its stamp lapsed while one dwarf was still live, calling reopened and started four more builders, and the store-in-RAM cutover aborted. The ruling was to wait for the named beads for at most 30 minutes, never to drain for 30 minutes.
