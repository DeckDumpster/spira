# §1.42 law-a-blinded-operator-surface-is-p0

A defect that blinds an operator surface — the ops pane, the asks pane, a status briefing's facts — is P0 whether or not the loop is still moving: the operator steers by that surface, and a loop he cannot see is one he cannot stop in time. Price it by what it hides from him, never by whether beads still land.
