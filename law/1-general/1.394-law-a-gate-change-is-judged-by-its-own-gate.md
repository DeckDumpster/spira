# §1.394 law-a-gate-change-is-judged-by-its-own-gate

A change to the gate own files (its definition, the gate crate, the fence scripts its steps run) enters a round only once its own gate has passed it at that tip; a no-verdict or a red keeps it out. Every other submitted item is admitted unjudged as before, because the round is the stronger trial. The gate is the one component a round cannot judge: a change that stops it producing verdicts looks, to the round, like any green member. Scar: sp-x1ftb4 wrapped the lint step, its own gate ended with no verdict, a round took it anyway, and the gate passed nothing for eight hours (ruling sp-nl8hu9).
