# §1.176 law-clock-reducers-go-straight-to-ci

While the next full round is still filling, a certified P0 bead that reduces the loop's clock time (test, gate, certification or landing wall time; labelled clock-reducer) does not wait for the pool. Under queue.forge it goes straight to CI as a single-member batch with no local corpus. Under queue.local every waiting clock-reducer goes as one fast-track round that runs the full local corpus and lands, because no CI stands between a local land and production. If it goes red, fix forward. Per Ryan, 2026-09-28; the queue.local clause was written by the harness and ratified by Ryan 2026-09-30 (sp-qczof).
