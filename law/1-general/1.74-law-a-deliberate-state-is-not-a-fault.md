# §1.74 law-a-deliberate-state-is-not-a-fault

A state the operator chose must never render as a fault. A halt, a drain, a parked persona roster, a stopped timer — each leaves subsystems idle, and a check that reads idleness as breakage fires for as long as the choice lasts, burying the real alarm and shipping advice from the wrong diagnosis that does damage the deliberate state never would. Read the switch before reporting the symptom: a parked subsystem is WAITING, and is reported as waiting or not at all.
