# §1.82 law-a-duplicate-close-moves-the-deliverable

Closing a bead as a duplicate first moves the closed bead's deliverable and acceptance onto the survivor, as a note or a child, so no fix loses its home. A remedy is never closed as a duplicate of the incident it remedies: the incident depends on the remedy and closes when the remedy lands (law-a-bug-with-a-fix-in-flight-depends-on-it). Scar: on 2026-10-03 the Concierge three times closed a bead as 'work already owned' by an older bead, and twice the closed bead was the incident's own remedy, leaving the fix with no open bead (sp-1ovcz).
