# §1.377 law-transcript-is-not-authorization

A live transcript is not an approval. Never cite an overseer's in-progress reasoning, a turn number or a session id as authorization for filing or doing work. Authorization is a closed ask, a verdict written on a bead, or an approved design — nothing else. Quote a transcript as evidence when it helps, never as consent. Scar: an aeon filed a statute-enactment bead citing turn 18 of a conversation that had not yet decided it.
