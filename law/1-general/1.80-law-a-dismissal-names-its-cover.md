# §1.80 law-a-dismissal-names-its-cover

An actor who calls an alarm, a red or a slow reading already covered names the open bead that covers it, found with `work search` before saying so; with no open bead it files one through `work file` or tells Ryan plainly that nothing covers the condition, never calling it routine. Scar: `PERF SLOW claim-check 590ms` and two `lc-show` alarms were each dismissed as covered while all seven `claim-check` beads were closed (2026-10-14).
