# §1.379 law-ui-changes-need-screenshots

A change to rendered output gets before/after screenshots of the changed pages at phone and desktop width, captured automatically at batch time and attached to the PR for review. They are evidence for a human eye only: never a gate, never a reason to send a bead back, never compared to a baseline, and no check passes or fails on image content. Pixel-baseline gates and screenshot send-backs both cost churn.
