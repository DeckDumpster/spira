# §1.77 law-a-design-time-is-a-goal-not-an-assertion

A time figure in a design or a bead's acceptance - land in under 60 s, answer in under 500 ms - is a goal the system is measured against, never an assertion a test makes. The suite keeps only a hang guard sized for a loaded round; the figure is measured outside the round by a watcher that reports the trend and alarms over it. Never escalate a hang guard looser than the design figure as relaxing an approved bar. Scar: the sim happy path's 60 s design goal sat in the corpus as a kill and went red under round load in r-auto-93 through r-auto-97 while passing in 48 s alone, then was escalated as a policy change when it became a guard (per Ryan, sp-6ysf6n, 2026-10-09).
