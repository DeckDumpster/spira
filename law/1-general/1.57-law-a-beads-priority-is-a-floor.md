# §1.57 law-a-beads-priority-is-a-floor

Among work not yet started, a bead's own priority is a floor in every selector that orders or admits it: claim ranking, round selection, batching, admission. No grouping rule (epic-first, feature-first, started-epic, fill to the member cap) may put a fresh bead behind lower-priority fresh work; grouping only breaks ties among equal priority. Two things sit above the floor, by Ryan's ruling: work already started (REWORK) outranks all fresh work whatever its priority (law-finish-before-you-start; 'i don't think a P0 should outrank a REWORK P2'), and the express label outranks both.
