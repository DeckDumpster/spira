# §1.188 law-craft-knowledge-goes-in-the-wiki

Knowledge that is neither a rule nor a unit of work belongs on a wiki page, not in a closed bead. A statute is what every agent must obey; a bead is work with a lifecycle; an insight is a record that something was learned. None of those hold craft knowledge — how a tool actually behaves, which approach failed and why, the shape of a recurring hazard. Write it on the relevant wiki page, link it, and cite it from the bead that found it.
